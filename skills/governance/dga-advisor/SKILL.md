# Digital Government Advisor Skill

Supporting government and semi-government digital delivery per DGA (Digital Government Authority) orientation: digital government maturity assessment, service transformation review, and digital-experience standards alignment. Procedures with verify-current discipline: DGA frameworks, maturity models, and service standards evolve - official texts (dga.gov.sa) are the authority.

## When to Use
- Supporting a government/semi-government entity's digital service initiatives
- Maturity assessments aligned with national digital government frameworks
- Service transformation roadmaps and digital excellence reviews
- Don't use for: data management detail (ndmo-advisor), security controls (nca-advisor), commercial-sector AI strategy (saudi-caio-advisor private path)

## Procedure
1. **Verify current frameworks.** Pull current DGA-published frameworks, maturity models, and service standards from official sources; note versions. Orientation memory (verify): DGA regulates and enables digital government in KSA, publishes digital government maturity assessment tools and service standards for government entities. Completion criterion: framework set + versions recorded.
2. **Maturity assessment.** Assess the entity against the current maturity model dimensions (service provision, data, technology, governance, user experience) with evidence per score; no evidence = unassessed. Completion criterion: scored assessment with artifacts.
3. **Service transformation review.** Per service: current journey, friction points, digital-readiness, automation/AI opportunity; prioritize by citizen-impact and feasibility. Completion criterion: service backlog scored.
4. **Standards alignment.** Check service design against current digital-experience/interoperability standards (verify text); note gaps. Completion criterion: standards-conformance checklist per service.
5. **Roadmap.** Sequenced transformation roadmap with capacity, dependencies, and measurement; feeds program-manager delivery. Completion criterion: roadmap with owners.
6. **Governance integration.** Findings route into saudi-caio-advisor dossier and ndmo-advisor data work - one stack. Completion criterion: links current.

## Quick Reference
- **DGA owns service experience and maturity; SDAIA owns data & AI; NCA owns security; CST owns telecom/ICT provisioning.** Route by ownership.
- **Government clients:** NDMO data instruments generally bind more strongly in public entities - applicability screening first.
- **User-first evidence:** maturity scores need user-research artifacts, not opinion scores.

## Pitfalls
- **Framework version drift:** maturity models are revised - assessments must state the version used.
- **Maturity theater:** high scores without operational evidence collapse under assessor review.
- **Private-sector overextension:** DGA instruments target government entities; private-sector applicability must be screened, not assumed.

## Verification
- Assessments cite framework versions; evidence per score.
- Roadmap tracked; standards checklist current.
- Quarterly re-verification against official DGA publications.
