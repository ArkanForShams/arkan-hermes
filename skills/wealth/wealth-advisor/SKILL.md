# Wealth Advisor Skill

Shariah-compliant wealth planning procedures: foundation-first structuring, goal mapping, halal vehicle comparison, zakat awareness, and portfolio review discipline. ANALYSIS ONLY - this skill plans and compares; it never executes transactions, never stores bank/card/payment details, and never issues fatwa-level rulings (Shariah-screening handles instrument research; qualified scholars rule; a licensed Shariah-compliant financial advisor is recommended for major decisions).

## When to Use
- Structuring financial foundations: emergency reserve, family security, future needs
- Comparing halal investment vehicles for a goal
- Annual/quarterly portfolio review and rebalancing analysis
- Zakat-eligible asset tracking support
- Don't use for: instrument compliance verdicts (shariah-screening + scholars), transaction execution (never), tax/legal opinions (qualified professionals)

## Procedure
1. **Foundation check first.** Before any investment talk: emergency reserve (target 3-6 months of family expenses, in accessible halal form), family security arrangements, near-term obligations listed. Giving and investing come FROM security, not before it. Completion criterion: reserve status stated with numbers before any vehicle discussion.
2. **Goal mapping.** Each goal: horizon (years), priority (essential/important/aspirational), target amount, monthly capacity. Typical map: children's education, family obligations, sadaqah/jariyah capacity, long-term independence. Completion criterion: goal table with horizons and amounts.
3. **Vehicle comparison (halal only).** Per goal-horizon, compare: screened equity funds/index funds (growth, volatility noted), sukuk (capital stability, lower return), real estate (income + stability, illiquidity), physical gold (preservation, no yield), direct halal business/partnership (highest effort, highest alignment). Completion criterion: comparison table per goal with risk, liquidity, effort, expected range - ranges as estimates with assumptions stated, never promises.
4. **Prohibited list (always on).** No riba (interest-bearing accounts, conventional bonds, interest-based leverage); no prohibited industries (alcohol, gambling, conventional insurance - takaful instead, and similar); no gharar (excessive-speculation instruments, gambling-like derivatives, day-trading mindset). Completion criterion: any proposed vehicle passes the prohibited list explicitly.
5. **Diversification discipline.** No single bet threatens family security; spread across asset classes and geographies within halal options; position sizing rules agreed in calm moments, applied in volatile ones. Completion criterion: allocation written with maximums per class.
6. **Zakat awareness.** Track zakat-eligible assets (cash, gold, invested wealth above nisab, held one lunar year); flag the calculation date; zakat is due on it at 2.5% (specific computation -> scholar/advisor for edge cases). Completion criterion: zakat-eligible asset list maintained with annual review date.
7. **Review cadence.** Quarterly: performance vs goals (not vs neighbors), rebalancing drift check, life-change scan (new child, family obligation, income change). Annual: full foundation re-check + goal refresh. Completion criterion: review log with decisions.
8. **Decision protocol.** Major decisions: analysis prepared -> istikhara -> decision rests with Shams; ARKAN never decides, never repeats disagreement after final call (disagreement protocol), and frames risk honestly before the decision, not after.

## Quick Reference
- **Order of operations:** reserve -> security -> goals -> vehicles -> diversification -> zakat -> review. Skipping steps 1-2 invalidates everything after.
- **Honest framing rule:** every projection carries assumptions and a range; anything promising fixed high returns with low risk is a red flag (likely scam or gharar).
- **Red flags checklist:** guaranteed returns, urgency pressure, unclear where money sits, returns from "interest arbitrage", unverifiable operators.
- **Wealth purpose ladder:** security -> family -> generosity -> legacy. Returns serve the ladder, not ego.
- **Documentation habit:** every decision memo saved with date, reasoning, and the alternatives rejected.

## Pitfalls
- **Return-chasing drift:** hot opportunities erode the prohibited list one "exception" at a time. The list is not negotiable.
- **Over-giving before securing:** generosity from an unsecured foundation creates hidden dependence (watch pattern - flag gently, decide is his).
- **Analysis paralysis:** perfect certainty never arrives; decide at "sufficient analysis + istikhara".
- **Speculation creep:** "long-term investment" redefined as trading after two good months. Horizon discipline.
- **Neglecting the hereafter lens:** wealth planning includes jariyah capacity (sadaqah, family support, knowledge) - not just accumulation.
