# Change Management Advisor Skill

Adoption and change procedures for IT initiatives: stakeholder mapping, communication plans, readiness assessment, training design, and resistance handling - so delivered systems actually get used.

## When to Use
- Planning adoption for a new system, process, or AI tool
- Stakeholder mapping, communication plans, readiness checks
- Post-launch adoption tracking and course correction
- Don't use for: delivery scheduling (program-manager), executive narrative (executive-reporting)

## Procedure
1. **Stakeholder map.** Grid: influence x impact -> Champion / Supporter / Neutral / Skeptic / Blocker, with a named engagement owner each. Completion criterion: map live; every Skeptic/Blocker has an owner and approach.
2. **Change impact assessment.** Per affected group: what changes for them, what they lose, what they gain, skills gap. Completion criterion: impact table per audience.
3. **Communication plan.** Audience x message x channel x moment (before/during/after); two-way channels included, not just announcements. Completion criterion: plan scheduled; feedback channel live.
4. **Readiness gates.** Before go-live: training done, support staffed, fallback known, champions active per team. Completion criterion: readiness checklist signed per team.
5. **Training design.** Role-based, task-based (do the job, not the features); champions trained first. Completion criterion: completion tracked per role.
6. **Adoption tracking.** Usage metrics from the system (logins, feature use, task completion); target adoption curve agreed at launch. Completion criterion: adoption dashboard reviewed weekly for 8 weeks post-launch.
7. **Resistance handling.** Listen for the real objection (workload, fear, competence, lost status); respond specifically; escalate blockers through the sponsor. Completion criterion: objections logged with responses.

## Quick Reference
- **What people lose matters more than what they gain;** name the losses honestly.
- **Champions beat memos:** one respected local advocate outperforms a campaign.
- **Adoption is a project metric,** not a hope: tracked like schedule and budget.
- **Go-live is the middle, not the end;** the 8 weeks after decide success.

## Pitfalls
- **Training as a mail-out:** nobody adopts from a PDF. Role-based, hands-on, champion-led.
- **Ignoring the Skeptics:** they become Blockers at go-live. Engage them before, not after.
- **One big bang comms:** attention spikes and dies. Plan the after-launch rhythm.
- **Declaring victory at launch:** usage data says otherwise within weeks. Track or assume failure.

## Verification
- Stakeholder map, comms plan, readiness checklists, adoption dashboard all exist.
- Adoption targets met or corrective actions logged within the first 8 weeks.
- Post-launch retrospective includes adoption lessons.
