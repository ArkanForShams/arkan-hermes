# Executive Coach Skill

Operational coaching procedures for executive presence: pre-meeting preparation, on-the-spot structure, respectful disagreement, post-meeting debriefs, and visibility planning. Complements SOUL.md coaching frameworks with concrete run sheets. Does not handle weekly planning cycles (weekly-review-planning) or meeting minutes (meeting-action-items).

## When to Use
- Preparing for an important meeting, briefing, or board exposure
- Rehearsing a difficult conversation or a diplomatic disagreement
- Building speaking presence; converting written strength into spoken authority
- Making contributions visible to leadership without self-promotion
- Don't use for: recurring weekly reviews, minute-taking, inbox triage

## Procedure
1. **Pre-meeting sheet (T-24h).** Write: my position in one sentence; three data points that support it; five likely questions with answers; 2-3 diplomatic phrases ready. Completion criterion: sheet exists and was read aloud once.
2. **Diplomatic phrase bank.** Select from: "I support the goal. Before we commit, may I raise one risk about X?" / "May I offer a different reading of the data?" / "Help me understand the constraint driving this timeline." / "Can we test this on a smaller scope first?" Completion criterion: phrases personalized to the actual meeting.
3. **On-the-spot structure.** When put on the spot: pause two seconds, acknowledge the question, answer in three points, stop. Practice: acknowledge → "Three things matter here..." → 1, 2, 3 → "That's my view." Completion criterion: rehearsed once before the meeting.
4. **During the meeting.** Speak within the first ten minutes (presence anchor); if disagreement was raised by others, support or challenge explicitly — silence reads as consent.
5. **Debrief (T+24h).** Capture: what went well, where I stayed silent and why, what to say next time, one skill to drill. Completion criterion: debrief written within 24 hours; the next-meeting prep sheet references it.
6. **Visibility loop.** After delivering results, send a short summary upward: outcome, numbers, next step — in that order. Completion criterion: win communicated within a week of delivery.
7. **Monthly skill focus.** One executive skill per month (e.g., saying no, on-the-spot answers, board Q&A). Completion criterion: named, drilled twice, reviewed at month end.

## Quick Reference
- **Saying no without guilt:** "I can't take this on and deliver my current commitments well. Which should I deprioritize?" — puts the trade-off on the asker.
- **Handling criticism:** separate the lesson from the sting the same day; write the one useful sentence, discard the rest, refocus on the next deliverable.
- **Meeting prep checklist:** position / 3 data points / 5 Q&A / 3 phrases / opener planned / debrief scheduled.
- **Progressive exposure ladder:** small internal sessions → team presentations → executive briefings → board/external.

## Pitfalls
- **Script-reading:** memorized verbatim answers collapse under follow-ups. Memorize positions and structures, not sentences.
- **Silence when the voice is needed:** the room belongs to whoever speaks; a prepared phrase breaks the silence barrier.
- **Over-loading:** more than three data points dilutes; executives remember three.
- **Skipping debriefs:** lessons evaporate in 48 hours; the debrief is where presence actually compounds.

## Verification
- Prep sheet predates the meeting; debrief logged after it.
- Diplomatic phrases used in the room at least once per month.
- Visibility summaries sent for each significant delivered outcome.
