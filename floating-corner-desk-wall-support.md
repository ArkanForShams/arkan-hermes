# Floating Corner Desk — Wall Support Addendum

## Desk geometry

- Two wall runs: 1050 mm each.
- Worktop depth: 550 mm.
- Top thickness: 25 mm.
- Finished height: 740 mm.
- Central concave knee opening: 700 mm wide.
- No floor legs.

## Recommended support system

### 1. Continuous rear ledger

Install a structural ledger along both walls:

- Minimum timber ledger: 75 × 50 mm structural timber, or
- Preferred concealed steel ledger/frame: 50 × 50 × 3 mm RHS.
- Run continuously along each 1050 mm wall length.
- Anchor spacing: 300–400 mm maximum.
- Put anchors close to both ends and around the inside corner.
- Ledger must be level before the worktop is fitted.

### 2. Cantilever brackets

Use four concealed triangular steel brackets:

- Two brackets on the left wall run.
- Two brackets on the right wall run.
- Bracket projection: 450 mm minimum.
- Bracket vertical drop: approximately 450 mm.
- Bracket material: 50 × 50 × 3 mm RHS or engineered equivalent.
- Wall plate: minimum 100 × 6 mm steel plate, subject to fabricator design.
- Bracket centres: approximately 150 mm and 900 mm from the inside corner on each wall run.
- Keep bracket faces behind the front edge and outside the 700 mm seated knee opening.
- Cover or recess all bracket edges so no metal can contact the knees or shins.

### 3. Corner reinforcement

Behind the 27-inch monitor arm:

- Add a concealed 50 × 50 × 3 mm steel L-frame or corner plate.
- Reinforce both walls for at least 300 mm around the monitor-arm mounting zone.
- Do not mount the monitor arm only into the 25 mm desktop.

### 4. Worktop connection

- Bond or screw the top to the rear ledger and steel frame.
- Use countersunk fixings from below where possible.
- Keep all fixing heads flush.
- Add a thin front fascia only if it does not reduce knee clearance.

## Protected knee and foot zone

- Central clear knee opening: 700 mm.
- Clear knee height: 650 mm minimum.
- No vertical panel, bracket, cable tray, or ledger projection into this opening.
- Foot rail width: 500 mm.
- Foot rail height: adjustable 120–180 mm above finished floor.
- Foot rail setback: 150 mm behind the front edge.
- Foot rail tube: 32–40 mm diameter, rounded or rubber-covered.

## Load target

Design target:

- 100 kg uniformly distributed working load across the complete platform.
- Monitor-arm zone must resist local movement and vibration.
- Do not sit, stand, climb, or lean heavily on the platform.

The 100 kg figure is a furniture design target, not a wall-anchor certification.

## Wall-specific installation decision

### Concrete or solid brick

Use appropriately rated M10 or M12 mechanical or chemical anchors with the embedment specified by the anchor manufacturer and installer.

### Hollow block

Use chemical anchors with approved sleeves or another system designed for hollow masonry. Do not use ordinary expansion anchors without verification.

### Stud wall / plasterboard

Do not anchor to plasterboard alone. Fix into at least two structural studs, or open the wall and install structural blocking/backing before closing it.

### Unknown wall

Stop and inspect before drilling. The carpenter must identify the wall type, locate structural members, select anchors, and sign off the load test.

## QC before equipment installation

1. Check both wall runs and the true 90-degree corner.
2. Confirm ledger is level within 2 mm across the full run.
3. Confirm all anchors are in structure, not plasterboard only.
4. Confirm no bracket enters the 700 mm knee opening.
5. Apply a gradual distributed test load before installing equipment.
6. Check for movement, creaking, anchor pull-out, or visible deflection.
7. Sit for 30 minutes and confirm knees, shins, and feet remain clear.
8. Install the monitor and laptops only after the support test passes.
