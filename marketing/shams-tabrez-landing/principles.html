Principles

What I stand on.

Six pillars hold up any career worth having.

I

Faith first

Sincere effort, gratitude for the outcome, and trust in Allah at the center of everything.

II

Family oneness

United relationships are real wealth. I work to keep the family one.

III

Honesty

Speak the truth or stay calm — never argue for its own sake.

IV

Discipline

Steady, consistent work — as my parents taught — beats any burst of intensity.

V

Peace

A peaceful life and a peaceful home. Ambition should serve that, never consume it.

VI

Service

Help people in their hard times — ideally before they have to ask.
