Privacy Policy — AI Meeting Assistant

Effective: October 2026 · Owner and data controller: Shams Tabrez · Contact: Shams090484@gmail.com

1. Scope

This application is a single-user, personal-use tool. It is not distributed to or usable by the public. The only Google-account data it accesses belongs to the app owner (Shams Tabrez) with his explicit authorization.

2. What data is accessed

  • Google Meet metadata and transcripts via the read-only scope meetings.space.readonly — conference records and transcript entries from meetings the owner himself attended or hosted.
  • No other Google services or scopes are accessed. No Gmail, Calendar, Drive, contacts, or media file data is read by this application.

3. Where data is stored

  • All fetched transcripts and generated outputs are stored locally on the owner's own computer, encrypted at rest (SQLCipher).
  • The application does not upload, share, sell, or transmit user data to any third-party server, advertising network, or AI-training service.
  • Google remains the original host of meeting artifacts; the app fetches copies and processing happens locally.

4. How data is used

  • Transcripts are processed by the owner's local software to produce summaries, minutes of meeting, decisions, and action-item lists for personal productivity.
  • Data is never used for advertising, profiling, model training, resale, or transfer to data brokers.
  • Data is retained only until the owner deletes it; deletion cascades to derived outputs and indexes.

5. User rights

Because the app is single-owner by design, all data belongs to and is controlled by the owner. He can inspect, export, or delete all stored data at any time through the local interface.
