# Changan-KSA Nationwide Dealer Agent Portal Rollout Target

Date recorded: 2026-10-11
Strategic target: Changan-KSA nationwide
Target start date: 2026-12-01
Status: Target plan; not production approval or launch commitment

## 1. Strategic decision

Changan-KSA nationwide is the strategic target for the Dealer Agent Operating Portal. The target start date is **December 1, 2026**.

This is a rollout target, not permission to bypass the existing production-readiness gates. The nationwide target must be delivered through controlled activation waves, with each wave dependent on security, privacy, operational, integration and support acceptance.

## 2. Product boundary

The target solution is the Dealer Agent Operating Portal:

- Full dealer-assisted corporate leasing lifecycle
- No minimum dealer DMS integration
- Dealer agents operate through the Auto Lead portal
- Individual customers excluded from this phase
- Direct corporate customer self-service excluded from the first release
- Synthetic data first
- Full workflow visible, with provider-backed actions gated until evidence is accepted
- AI Dealer Operations Copilot with human approval for consequential actions

## 3. Rollout principle

Do not launch the entire Changan-KSA network as one uncontrolled big-bang release. Use a nationwide target with phased activation:

1. **Foundation and readiness** — now through October 31, 2026
2. **Controlled activation preparation** — November 1–15, 2026
3. **Pilot-wave rehearsal and go/no-go** — November 16–30, 2026
4. **Target start of first approved activation wave** — December 1, 2026
5. **Progressive national expansion** — after evidence from the first wave is accepted

The first wave's exact branches, agent count and operating schedule remain to be confirmed by Auto Lead Operations and the National Sales Manager (Lease).

## 4. Required readiness gates before December 1

The target date cannot override these gates:

### Product and operating model

- Signed scope and non-goals
- Changan-KSA rollout waves and branch list
- Dealer onboarding and training plan
- Support, escalation and operating ownership
- Approved success metrics and go/no-go criteria

### Identity and access

- Dealer identity topology
- Agent, showroom manager and dealer administrator roles
- Tenant and fleet-pool authorization matrix
- Joiner/mover/leaver process
- MFA, revocation, privileged access and break-glass controls
- Negative authorization tests

### Data and privacy

- Approved field-level data inventory
- Purpose/minimization decisions
- Notices and consent/basis model
- Retention and legal-hold model
- Rights and deletion/anonymisation workflow
- Provider, residency and subprocessor evidence

### Operations and integrations

- Vehicle catalogue and availability operating procedure
- Rate publication and versioning procedure
- Reservation and conflict handling
- Provider/API evidence or approved stub boundaries
- Reconciliation and exception queues
- Incident, rollback and disaster-recovery runbooks

### Finance and compliance

- Approved synthetic pricing and VAT calculation examples
- Invoice and settlement boundary
- Payment and D365 posting decision
- No blind retry/unknown-outcome controls
- Qualified Finance, Tax, Compliance and Legal acceptance where applicable

### E-check and AI

- Synthetic e-check feasibility results
- Device and offline capture protocol
- Tamper, replay, wrong-scope and partial-upload tests
- AI risk classification and human approval gates
- AI evaluation set and monitoring plan
- No autonomous release, financial, credit, legal or compliance decision

## 5. Recommended activation waves

### Wave 0 — Internal synthetic rehearsal

Participants:

- Auto Lead Operations
- National Sales Manager (Lease)
- General Manager, AutoLead or delegate
- IT, Security, Privacy, Finance and Support

Purpose:

- Validate workflows with synthetic dealers, agents, companies, vehicles and contracts
- Exercise failure, pending, duplicate and reconciliation paths
- Validate Arabic/English user experience
- Prove support and escalation procedures

### Wave 1 — Controlled Changan-KSA activation

Recommended shape:

- A small representative set of Changan-KSA branches
- Limited number of named dealer agents
- Synthetic or explicitly approved controlled data only
- Feature flags for provider-backed actions
- Daily operational review during the first period
- Formal go/no-go review before adding additional branches

The branch list and agent count must be selected by Auto Lead Operations based on readiness and support capacity, not only commercial coverage.

### Wave 2 onward — Progressive national expansion

Expand by branch or region after the previous wave demonstrates:

- No unresolved critical tenant/security incident
- Acceptable workflow completion and error rates
- Reconciled vehicle, quote and contract states
- Support capacity within agreed response targets
- Approved privacy and data-handling evidence
- No uncontrolled financial or provider side effects

## 6. Success measures for the first activation wave

The exact targets require Operations approval, but the baseline measures should include:

- Agent onboarding completion rate
- Quote creation and conversion completion rate
- Time from customer intake to approved draft contract
- Missing-document and rework rate
- Vehicle availability conflict rate
- Workflow failure and pending-state ageing
- E-check completeness rate, if enabled
- Support tickets per active agent
- Authorization-denial and cross-tenant test results
- Reconciliation exceptions by age and owner
- AI suggestion acceptance and correction rate
- Zero confirmed cross-tenant data exposure
- Zero unapproved financial/provider side effects
