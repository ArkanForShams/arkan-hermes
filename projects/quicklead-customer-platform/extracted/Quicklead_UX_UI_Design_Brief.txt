QUICKLEAD
Customer App & B2B Portal - UX/UI Brief
Design Brief | Information Architecture, Screens, Journeys and Visual Direction
1. Design Objective
Create a fast, premium and simple automotive service experience. The customer should be able to 
understand the condition of each vehicle, book service in a few steps, follow the job without calling the 
branch, approve additional work and access invoices/rewards. The B2B portal should prioritize fleet visibility, 
approvals and financial control.
2. Design Principles
 Fast: the most common actions should require minimal taps.
 Vehicle-first: organize the experience around the customer's cars, not internal workshop terminology.
 Transparent: show status, price, recommendations and approvals clearly.
 Trustworthy: use real photos, clear service descriptions, invoice visibility and approval history.
 Bilingual: Arabic-first quality with full English support and correct RTL/LTR layouts.
 Consistent: one design system across mobile app and B2B portal.
3. Recommended B2C Navigation
Suggested bottom navigation: Home | My Garage | Book | Rewards | Account
 Home: next service, active booking/RO, quick booking, offers and reminders.
 My Garage: all vehicles, vehicle health, mileage, service history, recommendations and documents.
 Book: select vehicle, service, branch, slot, extras and confirmation.
 Rewards: points balance, tier, available rewards, coupons and referral.
 Account: profile, saved information, notifications, language, support and settings.

5. Main Customer Journeys
1. New customer: Onboarding -> OTP -> Add Vehicle -> Home -> Book Service.
2. Returning customer: Home -> Vehicle -> Book -> Branch -> Slot -> Confirm.
3. Active repair: Push notification -> Service Tracker -> View status -> Ready notification -> 
Payment/Invoice.
4. Additional work: Push notification -> Estimate -> Photo/details -> Approve/Reject -> Updated service 
tracker.
5. Retention: Service reminder -> Vehicle details -> Recommended service -> Booking -> Reward earned.
6. Vehicle Card / My Garage Concept
Each vehicle card should immediately answer: Which car is this? What is its current service status? What 
should I do next? Recommended elements: vehicle image/illustration, make/model/year, plate, mileage, last 
service, next service, open recommendation and a clear Book Service CTA.
Optional future concept: Vehicle Health Score (e.g., 87/100), supported by transparent factors rather than a 
unexplained score.
7. Service Tracker Concept

 Vehicle Checked In

 Completed
8. B2B Portal Information Architecture
Suggested left navigation: Dashboard | Vehicles | Work Orders | Approvals | Invoices | Statements | Reports 
| Users & Settings
 Dashboard: KPIs, vehicles currently in workshop, pending approvals, overdue items and spend trend.
 Vehicles: searchable fleet table with vehicle profile and complete maintenance history.
 Work Orders: open/closed ROs, status, branch, dates and cost.
 Approvals: prioritized queue with amount, vehicle, requested work, SLA/age and decision controls.
 Invoices & Statements: filters, status, due date, PO reference, download/export.
 Reports: spend by vehicle/branch/service, downtime, frequency and exceptions.
 Users & Settings: roles, approval limits, cost centers and notification rules.
9. Visual Direction
 Modern automotive look: clean surfaces, strong typography, generous spacing and high-contrast CTAs.
 Use Quicklead brand colors as the base; create a documented color, typography, icon and component 
system.
 Photography/illustration should focus on vehicles, technicians, quick service and trust - avoid generic 
corporate stock imagery.
 Status colors/icons must remain accessible and never rely on color alone.
 Cards should surface the next action rather than overload users with technical data.
 Design mobile screens first, then responsive B2B desktop/tablet layouts.
10. Components to Design
 Buttons, inputs, OTP, search, filters, dropdowns and date/time selectors.
 Vehicle cards, service cards, branch cards and offer/reward cards.
 Status chips, progress timeline, alerts and notification cards.
 Price/estimate component with VAT and total.
 Approval component with photos, comments and approve/reject actions.
 Tables, pagination, KPI cards, charts and export controls for B2B.
 Empty states, loading states, errors, offline/retry and success confirmations.
11. Required Design Deliverables
 User flows for B2C and B2B.
 Information architecture / sitemap.
 Low-fidelity wireframes.
 High-fidelity Arabic and English screens.
 Clickable prototype for booking, active service, approval and B2B approval flows.

 Responsive B2B portal designs.
 Design system and reusable component library.
 Developer handoff with states, spacing, assets and interaction notes.
12. Priority Prototype Scope
For the first design review, prototype these four journeys end-to-end: (1) login and add vehicle, (2) book a 
periodic service, (3) follow an active service and approve additional work, (4) B2B fleet manager reviews 
and approves a work request.
