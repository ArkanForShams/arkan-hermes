# Compact Floating Corner WFH Platform — Carpenter Build Sheet

**Revision:** 1.0
**Purpose:** Compact ergonomic corner platform for one 27-inch LCD monitor and two laptops.
**Units:** millimetres.

## 1. Final design

Use a floating L-corner platform with:

- Two equal wall runs: **1050 mm each** from the inside room corner.
- Worktop depth: **550 mm**.
- Finished desktop height: **740 mm from finished floor**.
- Top thickness: **25 mm**.
- Front edge: straight outer edges with small chamfers; central concave knee opening.
- No floor legs, pedestal, vertical side panels, or exposed knee brackets.
- 27-inch LCD mounted on a monitor arm at the inside corner.
- One laptop on each side wing.
- Small mousepad zone beside each laptop.
- Recessed wall-mounted foot rail or foot shelf.

## 2. Top-panel geometry

### Overall

- Left wall edge: **1050 mm**.
- Right wall edge: **1050 mm**.
- Back-to-front depth: **550 mm**.
- Inside corner: true 90-degree corner, measured after wall finishing.
- Keep the rear 100 mm clear for cable routing and mounting hardware.

### Front edge

- Left and right outside front corners: **150 × 150 mm, 45-degree chamfers**.
- No sharp exposed corners.
- Round all user-facing edges to **R8 minimum**, preferably **R12**.
- Create a central concave knee opening:
  - Opening width at the front: **700 mm**.
  - Opening depth into the platform: **180 mm**.
  - Use a smooth curve, not a square cutout.
  - Curve radius: approximately **350 mm**.
  - Tangent the curve into the left and right straight front edges.
- The seated position is centred on this opening.

### Recommended cut shape

From the inside corner along each wall, mark 1050 mm. Worktop depth is 550 mm. At the front edge, chamfer each outside corner 150 × 150 mm. Between the two chamfers, cut a smooth 700 mm wide concave opening that travels 180 mm toward the rear. Sand and round the entire opening.

## 3. Equipment zones

### Monitor

- 27-inch 16:9 LCD panel envelope: approximately **628 × 366 mm** including bezel allowance.
- Mount on a monitor arm clamped or bolted near the inside corner.
- Monitor centre should be approximately **550–650 mm from the seated eye position**.
- Top of screen should be at or slightly below eye level.
- Keep the monitor arm base behind the monitor line so it cannot strike the head or knees.

### Laptops

- Left laptop zone: **360 × 260 mm**.
- Right laptop zone: **360 × 260 mm**.
- Place each laptop zone 80–100 mm from the respective outside edge.
- Keep at least 40 mm clearance from the monitor arm zone.
- Use laptop stands approximately 100–130 mm high.
- Use external keyboards and mice for long sessions.

### Mousepads

- Left mousepad zone: **220 × 180 mm**.
- Right mousepad zone: **220 × 180 mm**.
- Keep mousepads beside, not directly in front of, laptop stands.
- Maintain at least 100 mm from the curved front edge.

## 4. Ergonomic height and clearance

- Finished desktop height: **740 mm**.
- Underside of top: approximately **715 mm** above floor.
- Minimum clear knee height: **650 mm**.
- Clear seated width: **700 mm minimum**.
- No vertical support directly below the 700 mm knee opening.
- No bracket, cable tray, shelf, or footrest may project into the shin path.
- Target seated elbow angle: approximately 90 degrees.

## 5. Leg-rest options

### Recommended: recessed rounded foot rail

- Width: **500 mm**.
- Tube diameter: **32–40 mm**.
- Height: adjustable between **120 and 180 mm above finished floor**.
- Recess from front edge: **150 mm minimum**.
- Centre it on the seated opening.
- Use a rounded wooden or rubber-covered rail.
- Fix it to the rear wall cleat or hidden side brackets, never to a knee-facing front panel.

### Alternative: floating foot shelf

- Width: **500 mm**.
- Depth: **220 mm**.
- Height: **80–120 mm above floor**.
- Front edge radius: **R15 minimum**.
- Recess the front edge at least **150 mm behind the desk front edge**.
- Leave open space on both sides so the feet can move naturally.

### Ergonomic rule

Use the leg rest as a movable support, not as a fixed bar that traps both feet. You should be able to move each foot, change posture, and withdraw your legs without hitting hardware.

## 6. Floating support system

The carpenter must not rely on the 25 mm top alone.

### Rear wall cleat

- Continuous cleat along both wall runs.
- Recommended cleat: **75 mm high × 50 mm deep** minimum.
- Use structural timber or a welded steel angle frame behind the top.
- Fix into wall studs, reinforced blocking, or solid masonry.

### Hidden brackets

- Use concealed heavy-duty brackets behind the rear 100–150 mm zone.
- Minimum recommendation: **three supports per 1050 mm wing**, subject to wall construction.
- Brackets must be rated for at least **75 kg each** after installation.
- Brackets must not enter the 700 mm knee opening.
- Add corner reinforcement behind the monitor arm location.

### Important wall check

Before fabrication, confirm whether the wall is:

- Reinforced concrete
- Solid brick/block
- Stud wall with timber/metal studs
- Drywall/plasterboard over a cavity

Do not install a floating desk into plasterboard alone. Anchor into studs, structural blocking, or masonry using the correct anchors.

## 7. Material and finish

Recommended:

- 25 mm birch plywood, hardwood, or high-quality laminated board.
- 2 mm edge banding.
- Matte charcoal, dark walnut, or natural wood finish.
- R8–R12 rounded user-facing edges.
- Satin or matte surface to reduce glare.
- Two 60 mm cable grommets, one near each rear wing.
- Rear cable channel: approximately **100 mm high × 80 mm deep**.

## 8. Carpenter cut list

- Main top, left section: **1050 × 550 × 25 mm**.
- Main top, right section: **1050 × 550 × 25 mm**.
- Joint/corner reinforcement: hidden, minimum **600 × 600 mm** backing area.
- Rear wall cleat: continuous on both walls, **75 × 50 mm minimum**.
- Foot rail: **500 mm wide**, 32–40 mm diameter, adjustable 120–180 mm high.
- Optional foot shelf: **500 × 220 × 25 mm**.
- Two cable grommets: **Ø60 mm**.

## 9. Quality-control checklist before installation

- [ ] Room corner is measured at floor and desktop height.
- [ ] Both wall runs are at least 1050 mm usable length.
- [ ] Wall type and anchor locations are confirmed.
- [ ] Top is level within 2 mm across the full width.
- [ ] Concave opening is 700 mm wide minimum.
- [ ] No support enters the knee opening.
- [ ] No sharp edge remains after sanding.
- [ ] Monitor arm does not contact the wall or screen edge.
- [ ] Two laptops and two mousepads fit without overlap.
- [ ] Foot rail is recessed and cannot strike the shin.
- [ ] All cable holes are smooth and grommeted.
- [ ] Platform passes a static distributed-load test before use.

## 10. Acceptance test

Before placing equipment:

1. Apply a distributed test load gradually across both wings.
2. Check for movement, creaking, wall-anchor movement, or deflection.
3. Keep the user’s knees and shins inside the 700 mm opening while testing the chair position.
4. Sit for 30 minutes and check that knees, thighs, shins, and feet do not contact the desk, brackets, cleat, cable tray, or foot rail.
5. Confirm the monitor and laptop screens can be positioned without leaning forward.
6. Only then install the monitor, laptops, and cable equipment.

**Safety boundary:** The dimensions are build-ready for the furniture geometry, but the wall anchors and bracket sizes must be verified against the actual wall structure by the carpenter/installer. No responsible design can certify those loads without seeing the wall construction.
