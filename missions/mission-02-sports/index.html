01 — The Lab

Gear that
measures fast.

Our Portland lab runs every material decision through a 12-camera motion capture array and a force-plate treadmill before it earns a stitch. If it doesn't beat the control, it doesn't ship.

T/01

KineticFoam™

Supercritical midsole

A nitrogen-infused supercritical foam, twin-foamed for rebound without collapse. Tuned in 14 durometers until the return curve matched sprint-stage force data.

  • Energy return 87.2%
  • Density 0.11 g/cm³
  • Contact time delta −3.4%
  • Heat breakdown @40°C <2%
T/02

CarbonDrive Plate

Full-length propulsion

One-piece aerospace carbon laid on a variable-stiffness map — stiffest under the metatarsal hinge, compliant at heel strike. Tuned per event, not per marketing calendar.

  • Stiffness index 9.2 N/mm²
  • Running economy +4.2%
  • Plate mass 38g
  • Curvature radius R/11

Engineered zonal knit

A zonal engineered knit mapped from 27,000+ lab scans: locked where force demands it, ventilated where heat builds. 72% of current-generation yarn is recycled.

  • Weight (size 42) 212g

  • Recycled content 72%
  • Stretch recovery 96%
