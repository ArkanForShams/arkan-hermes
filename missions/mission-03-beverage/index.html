Our Story

One spring in Valencia.
A house of brands, thirty-eight years later.

In 1987, the Costa family began bottling water from a limestone spring in the Sierra Calderona north of Valencia, delivering it to beach cafés in a single repurposed van. The water had a mineral signature people remembered — and the habit of remembering became a business.

Today Meridian is a global house of five brands sold in 42 markets, run by the third generation of the family alongside 4,800 colleagues. We press our own citrus in Valencia, brew our teas in Sri Lanka and Kenya, and bottle within three days of harvest wherever the fruit allows.

We are a house of brands built entirely without alcohol — by conviction, not by omission. Every litre we bottle is one a family, a faith community, or a mindful drinker can share without a second thought.

That conviction is also a competitive engine: it makes Meridian the premium non-alcoholic choice for family occasions and faith-conscious consumers across the Gulf, Southeast Asia and beyond — markets where we grow fastest precisely because of what we refuse to make.

Water stewardship

We replenish 120% of the water we use across all 11 hubs — 2.9 billion litres returned in FY2025 through wetland restoration in Albufera, aquifer recharge in Sharjah and spring protection in Kericho.

120% replenished · AWS Gold certified at 6 hubs

rPET & packaging

87% of our plastic is rPET today; the remainder leaves the system entirely by January 2027. Glass is 62% recycled; aluminium cans carry a 15% lighter wall than 2019.

87% rPET now → 100% by 2027

Solar bottling

14.2 MWp of installed photovoltaics across Valencia, Agadir and Sharjah covered 41% of daytime production energy last year; the remaining grid supply is certified renewable (I-REC).

Where we're behind — said plainly

Two hubs (Tangerang and Rotterdam) still buy ~12% grid electricity we cannot yet certify renewable. Glass return rates in the Americas sit at 31% against our 50% target, and our Scope 3 dairy-adjacent logistics for Verda remain unmeasured at ingredient level. We report both gaps in the annual sustainability letter — same letter, same place, every year.
